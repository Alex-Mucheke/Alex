## 🚀 Selected Engineering Projects

### 📦 SendIT — Logistics & Parcel Delivery Platform

A logistics platform designed to improve parcel management, delivery coordination and real-time tracking.

- Parcel registration and lifecycle management.
- Customer and delivery workflows.
- Real-time parcel location updates.
- Operational dashboards.
- API integration and WebSocket communication.

**Stack:** Angular · NestJS · PostgreSQL · WebSockets

### 🛒 Supermarket Management System

A business management solution that brings supermarket operations into one centralized application.

- Point of Sale and sales processing.
- Inventory and stock management.
- Order management.
- Profit and business analytics.
- Employee and payroll management.

**Stack:** React · TypeScript · Node.js · Express · MySQL

### 🎓 CBC Learning Platform

An education platform focused on digital learning, assessments and student performance management.

- Digital learning resources.
- Examinations and assessments.
- AI-assisted learning.
- Student performance tracking.
- Parent, teacher and student workflows.

**Stack:** Next.js · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · AI integrations

### 🤝 Konnect — Worker & Job Matching Platform

A marketplace concept designed to connect workers with customers and businesses seeking services.

- Worker profiles and service discovery.
- Job matching and search.
- Customer and worker workflows.
- Location-based discovery.

**Stack:** React · TypeScript · Node.js
